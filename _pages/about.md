I am a PhD student in the Nuclear Theory Group, Department of Physics, The University of Tokyo. My advisor is Prof. Kenji Fukushima.

## Research

I work in nuclear and hadron theory on two topics.

**Nucleon structure.** I study the nucleon in the chiral quark soliton model. I compute generalized parton distributions (GPDs) of the nucleon at nonzero skewness and nonzero momentum transfer, summing the contributions of all quark states, including the Dirac continuum ([arXiv:2609.37354](/publication/2026-gpd-chiral-quark-soliton)). I also study how the chiral anomaly changes the decomposition of the nucleon spin into quark helicity and orbital angular momentum ([arXiv:2609.25867](/publication/2026-chiral-anomaly-spin)).

**Compact stars.** I study observables that distinguish quark stars from neutron stars. The magnetic-type Love number distinguishes quark stars from neutron stars that have similar masses and radii ([Phys. Rev. D 114, L061305](/publication/2026-magnetic-love-number)). I also study the origin of the universal relations among neutron-star observables by asymptotic analysis of the stellar structure equations ([arXiv:2608.19939](/publication/2026-universal-relations)).

My master's thesis, *Probing the Existence of Quark Stars via Tidal Response*, received the [Ukakuren Master's Thesis Award](https://www.cns.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/ukakuren/whatsnew/index.html) in 2026.
