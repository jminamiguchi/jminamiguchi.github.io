I am a PhD student in the Nuclear Theory Group, Department of Physics, The University of Tokyo. My advisor is Prof. Kenji Fukushima.

## Research interests

* Nucleon structure: generalized parton distributions and the nucleon spin in the chiral quark soliton model
* Compact stars: tidal Love numbers of quark stars and neutron stars

My master's thesis, *Probing the Existence of Quark Stars via Tidal Response*, received the [Ukakuren Master's Thesis Award](https://www.cns.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/ukakuren/whatsnew/index.html) in 2026.
